## The short version

WhatPing is free, in beta, and provided as-is. It is built and run by one person. It may go
down, miss a check, or fail to deliver an alert. Do not make it the only thing standing between
you and a serious outage.

## The service

WhatPing monitors endpoints and domains you configure, and sends alerts when a monitor changes
state. It is provided free of charge. There is no paid tier.

## No SLA and no warranty

There is **no uptime commitment, no availability guarantee and no support commitment.**

Concretely, and worth reading rather than skipping: a check may be missed. An alert may not be
delivered. The service may be unavailable when your service goes down. No compensation is
offered for any of this, and none is implied by the fact that it usually works.

If your monitoring must be contractually reliable, use a vendor who will sign something. The
[Better Stack comparison](/vs/better-stack) is honest about where that line falls.

## Acceptable use

Monitor things you own or are authorised to monitor. WhatPing fetches URLs at intervals you set,
and using it to generate load against a third party, to probe infrastructure you do not control,
or to circumvent access controls is not permitted.

Accounts doing any of the above may be suspended without notice.

## Your account

You are responsible for your credentials and for what your workspace members do. Passwords must
meet a minimum strength requirement and email verification is required.

## Changes

These terms and the service itself may change. Material changes will be announced on the

switched off without notice.

## Ending it

You can delete your account whenever you like. WhatPing may suspend or terminate an account for
the acceptable-use reasons above, or discontinue the service entirely — in which case you will
get as much notice as circumstances allow.

## Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, WhatPing is not liable for any loss arising from use of
the service, including any outage it failed to detect or alert on. It is a free beta tool
