No sales team, no demo booking, no chat widget in the corner. There is one developer and an
email address.

---

## Something is broken

Email **hello@whatping.com** with what you did, what happened, and the monitor name if it's specific to
one. If it's an outage on WhatPing itself, say so in the subject line.

## A security issue

Email **hello@whatping.com** rather than opening a public issue. You will get a reply, and credit if you
want it. See [security](/security) for what is already defended.

## A feature request

Email, or check the [roadmap](/roadmap) first — it may already be there, and it also lists what
is deliberately not planned, which will save you the message.

The most useful feature requests describe the failure you had, not the feature you want.

## A question about whether it fits

The pages most likely to answer it:

- [Pricing](/pricing) — including the limits and the absence of an SLA
- [WhatPing vs Uptime Kuma](/vs/uptime-kuma)
- [WhatPing vs Better Stack](/vs/better-stack)
- [Docs](/docs)

If the answer is that WhatPing doesn't fit, the comparison pages say which tool does. That is a
better outcome than a signup that turns into a disappointment.
